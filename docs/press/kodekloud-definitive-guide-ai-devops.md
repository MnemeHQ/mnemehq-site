## Contributed passages, as published

> As teams move from code generation to autonomous agents, the question is no longer
> whether agents can write code. It's whether they can consistently make engineering
> decisions that remain aligned with architectural intent.

> The future of engineering guardrails is making that intent machine-readable,
> enforceable, and continuously available wherever development occurs. As organizations
> deploy more AI coding agents, they need mechanisms that preserve architecture,
> engineering standards, security practices, operational reliability, compliance
> requirements, institutional knowledge, and cross-team consistency. Otherwise,
> development may accelerate while systems become increasingly fragmented, difficult to
> maintain, and disconnected from the intent of the engineering organization and
> ultimately the business objectives.
